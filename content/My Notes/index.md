Welcome to My-Notes.

I made these notes while preparing for my own senior secondary exams, so I thought instead of keeping them locked in my folders, I should just upload them here for everyone.

The notes are written in simple language and normal tone, not in that overly formal textbook style. I’ve tried to explain topics the way I personally understand them. Along with theory, I’ve also added derivations for most formulas and equations at CBSE level because those are usually the most confusing part while studying.

Right now, the site mainly contains complete Physics notes and Physical Chemistry chapters. Organic Chemistry is still missing because honestly I never got comfortable enough with it to make proper notes on it.

I’ll keep updating and managing this site in future whenever I add better notes or new subjects.
