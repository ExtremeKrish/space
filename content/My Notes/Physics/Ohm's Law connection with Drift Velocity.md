We know that
$$
V_{d} = \frac{eE}{m}\tau
$$
And also that
$$
I = neA \, V_{d}
$$
SO :
$$
\begin{gather*}
I = neA \, V_{d} \\
I = neA \times \frac{eE}{m}\tau \\
I = ne^2A \times \frac{V}{\ell} \times \frac{\tau}{m} \\ \\
\text{After rearrangement} \\
V = I \times \frac{\ell m}{ne^2A \tau}
\end{gather*}
$$
And that is kinda similar to 
$$
V = IR
$$
I.e. **OHM'S LAW**
