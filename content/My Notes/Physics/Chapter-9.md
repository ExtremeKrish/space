# Ray Optics
**Optics** : Study of Light
## Reflection
### Laws of Reflection
1. The Angle of Incidence = Angle of Reflection
2. The Incident Ray, The Reflected Ray & the normal lie on the same plane
### Reflection by Spherical Surfaces

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}}
$$
Where f = focal length | -ive in concave & +ive in convex
u = object distance | (-ive in both mirrors)
v = image distance (+/- in concave, +ive in convex)
$$
\boxed{m = \frac{h_{i}}{h_{o}} = \frac{-v}{u}}
$$
## Refraction
Phenomenon in which *direction of popagration of light changes at boundary, when it passes from one medium to other.*
- Rarer => $\mu_{1}$ & Denser => $\mu_{2}$ then $\mu_{1} < \mu_{2}$
	- $\lambda \downarrow \, v \downarrow \, \angle r \downarrow$ 
-  Denser => $\mu_{1}$ & Rarer => $\mu_{2}$ then $\mu_{1} > \mu_{2}$
	- $\lambda \uparrow \, v \uparrow \, \angle r \uparrow$ 
- $\nu$  (frequency) = constant

### Refractive Index (Optical Density)
**Relative**
$$
\mu_{y,x} = \frac{v_{x}}{v_{y}} = \frac{\lambda_{x}}{\lambda_{y}}
$$
**Absolute** (relative to Air/Vaccum)
$$
\mu_{m} = \frac{C}{V_{m}}
$$
### Snell's Law
$$
\mu_{x} \sin i = \mu_{y} \sin r
$$
$$
\boxed{\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{\mu_{y}}{\mu_{x}}} = \mu_{y,x} = \frac{v_{x}}{v_{y}} = \frac{\lambda_{x}}{\lambda_{y}}
$$
