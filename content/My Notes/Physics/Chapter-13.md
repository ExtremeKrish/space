**Nuclei**

- Atomic Number is represented by Z (which is number of Protons $n_{P}$)
- Mass Number is represented by A (which is the addition of $n_{P}$ and $n_{N}$  means neutrons)
- So Number of Neutrons = A - Z

**Neuclons** = A = $n_{p} + n_{n}$

**Atomic Mass Unit (amu)**
$$
1u = \frac{\text{mass of one } _{12}C \text{ atom}}{12}
$$
$$
1u = 1.66 \times 10^{-27} \; kg
$$
- $m_{e} = 9.11 \times 10^{-27}\; kg$
- $m_{p} \approx m_n = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}kg$
## Nuclear Radius (R)
$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Volume of Nucleus} &\propto \text{Mass No. (A)} \\
\frac{4}{3} \pi R^3 &\propto A \\
R^3 &\propto A \\
R &\propto A^{1/3} \\
R &= R_{0}A^\frac{1}{3} \\
\end{align*}
$$
Where $R_{0} = 1.2 f = 1.2 \times 10^{-15} \; m$
$$
\boxed{R = (1.2f) A^\frac{1}{3}}
$$
## Nuclear Density
$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Density} &= \frac{\text{Mass of Neucleons}}{\text{Volume of Nucleus}} \\
&= \frac{m_{nucleons} \times A}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3} \\
&= \frac{mA}{\frac{4}{3}\pi \left( R_{0}A^\frac{1}{3} \right)^3} \\
&= \frac{m}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R_{0}^3} \\
&= 2.3 \times 10^{17}\; kg/m^3
\end{align*}
$$
Which is so much as u can see, its even higher then iron or any metal.

## Energy-Mass Conversion

E = mc^2

## IsotoPes
↪ Same number of Protons i.e. Z same but different Mass Number (A)
E.g. $H_{1}^1,\;H_{1}^2,\;H_{1}^3$
## IsotoNes
↪ **Same number of Neutrons N = (A-Z)**
E.g. $_{80}Hg^{198},\;_{79}Au^{197}$
## IsobArs
↪ **Same number of Mass Number (A)**  but different Atomic Number/Proton Count/Z
E.g. $_{18}Ar^{40},\;_{20}Ca^{40}$ or $_{1}H^{3},\;_{2}He^{3}$ are Isobars
