# Contributing to My-Notes

If you also make good notes and want to share them here, you can contribute.

The goal of this site is simple:
easy notes, understandable language, proper derivations, and less textbook headache.

You do NOT need “perfect topper notes”. If your notes genuinely help someone understand a topic better, they are welcome.

---

## Important

This website is built on Quartz and uses Markdown files for everything.

So currently I only accept notes written in Markdown (`.md`) format.

If your notes are in PDF, DOCX, handwritten pages, screenshots, etc., you can still mail them to me, but there’s a chance I may not upload them unless they are converted properly.

---

## What can you contribute?

* Physics notes
* Chemistry notes
* Maths notes
* Short notes / revision sheets
* Derivations
* Diagrams
* PYQ solutions
* Helpful explanations for confusing topics

---

## How to contribute

### Option 1 — Easy Method

Just mail me your Markdown notes.

Include:

* Subject
* Chapter/topic name
* Your name or nickname (optional, for credits)

Email: [krishvishwakarma83@gmail.com](mailto: krishvishwakarma83@gmail.com)

I’ll review the notes and upload them.

---

### Option 2 — GitHub Method

If you know GitHub basics:

1. Fork the repository
2. Add your Markdown files
3. Commit the changes
4. Open a Pull Request

I’ll review it and merge it if everything looks good.

---

## Small request

Please upload only notes made by you or notes you have permission to share.
Don’t upload copyrighted coaching material or copied content.

Also try to keep explanations simple and understandable. The point of this site is clarity, not sounding overly academic.

---

Thanks to everyone contributing here.
Even a small contribution can help someone during exams.
