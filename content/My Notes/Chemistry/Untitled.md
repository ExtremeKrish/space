## **Coordination Compound Structure: $K_4[Fe(CN)_6]$**

---

| **Component** | **Description** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Counter Ion** | $K_4$ (Potassium ions outside the coordination sphere) |
| **Coordination Entity** | $[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-}$ (The central metal and its ligands enclosed in brackets) |
| **CMA / CMI** | **Fe** (Central Metal Atom / Central Metal Ion) |
| **Ligands** | **CN** (Cyanide groups bonded to the central metal) |
| **Coordination No.** | **6** (The total number of chemical bonds formed with the ligands) |

---

### **Visual Breakdown**

$$K_4 \underbrace{[ \overbrace{Fe}^{\text{CMA/CMI}} \quad \underbrace{(CN)}_{\text{Ligands}} \quad \overbrace{6}^{\text{Coord. No.}} ]}_{\text{Coordination Entity}}$$

* **$K_4$** $\leftarrow$ **Counter Ion**
* **$[Fe(CN)_6]$** $\leftarrow$ **Coordination Entity**
* **$Fe$** $\leftarrow$ **CMA (Central Metal Atom) / CMI (Central Metal Ion)**
* **$CN$** $\leftarrow$ **Ligands**
* **$6$** $\leftarrow$ **Coordination Number**
