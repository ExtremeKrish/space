We call D block elements: Transition Metals
& F block Elements: Inert Transition metals

F block elements are *between* atomic number 56  and 72 (Hafnium) and 88 to 104 (Rutherfordium) thus start from 57 (Lanthanum) to 71 (Lutetium) and 89 (Actinium) to 103 (Lawrencium).

**4D Series**
- Y, Zr, Nb, Mo, Tc, Rh, Pd, Ag, Cd
**5D Series** (Lanthenides)
- La, Hf, Ta, W, Re, Os, Ir, Pt, Au, Hg
**6d Series** (Actenides) (Radioactive) 
- Ac, Rf

## Electronic Configurations of the d-Block Elements 
You should know the New Electronic Configuration method & not that old 9th class one, this one :$1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6 4s^2 3d^{10}$
1. For 3d Series, configuration is $[Ar]\; 4s^2 \;3d^{1 \to 10}$ 
	1. But for ${}_{24}Cr \to 4s^1 \; 3d^5$ 
	2. For ${}_{29} Cu \to 4s^1 \; 3d^{10}$ 
2. For 4d Series, configuration is $[Ar] 5s^2 4d^{1 \to 10}$
	1. But Exceptions are also there for Nb, Mo, Tc, Ru, Rh, Pd, Ag 
3. For 5d series, configuration is $[Ar]\; 6s^2\; 5d^{1 \to 10}$
	1. But Exceptions are also there for Pt, Au

So the solution to remember the config is $[\text{Inert Gas}] \; ns^2\;(n-1)d^{1 \to 10}$ where n is Series Number minus 1, for Normal Elements,
And the configuration for BKL Exeption elements is $[\text{Inert Gas}]\; ns^1\; (n-1)d^{1 +1 \to 10+1}$ 
For Palladium -> s is 0 and d is 10.
