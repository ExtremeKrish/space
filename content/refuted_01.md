## Hanuman Footprint
This is a so-called Hanuman **footprint** in Thailand. Now you don't have to be a rock expert to know that if a mythically giant will step on rocks, there will be cracks around it.It would not form a perfectly chiseled footprint like this. This is called a questionable cause fallacy where you forcibly conclude that one thing caused the another simply because they are regularly associated, almost always without evidence. Also here are the footprints of many primates including humans.Which of these resemble the one in Thailand? None. 
## Ram Setu
Ram Setu's myth has been debunked by a joint study between ISRO and NASA where it has been conclusively proved that Ram Setu is not a man-made structure as it is told but it is a conglomerate of coral reefs and limestone shoals both of which are naturally occurring sediments. 

## Om sound in Black Hole
There is no sound of a black hole. This WhatsApp forward was born in 2022 when NASA released a sound of a black hole. But NASA also said that the sound they have released is of a galaxy cluster in which sound was generated due to the pressure waves of the black hole.
## Ravana's Palace
![|567x377](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4c/Beauty_of_Sigiriya_by_Binuka.jpg)

The Sri Lankan Sigiriya rock is often appropriated as Ravan's palace but the truth is that it was the 5th century capital of King Kashyapa I of Maurya dynasty. After the king's death, the rock was abandoned and used as a Buddhist monastery until the 14th century, the remnants of which are still prevalent in the rock.

## Dwarka City Underwater
This is a tricky one. The 1980 excavation by archaeologist S R Rao confirms to a great detail that the Samaj city of Dwarka may very well be the city that is mentioned in Mahabharata. But we must proceed with caution as the divinity of the structure is not known and will probably never be known. Historian Abdul Samad in his book Emergence of Hinduism in Gandhar agrees that Vasudev Krishna may have been a clan leader of historical authenticity who later got deified for his deeds as is done in many pagan religions. The crux of the matter is that there is still very little archaeological material about the submerged city and any conclusions with the myth itself are unwarranted.

However the Images which are being circulated online claiming that they're of official underwater dwarka are fake.
