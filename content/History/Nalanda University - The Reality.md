Nalanda Mahavira was one of the biggest educational institute of India in 5th-12th century. It was established in the 5th Century by Emperor Kumaragupta I, probably the world's first residential university, located in modern-day Bihar, India. It served as a premier center for Buddhist & Vedic learning for over 800 years, hosting 10,000 students and 2,000 teachers before its destruction.
